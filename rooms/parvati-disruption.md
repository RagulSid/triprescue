# Room instructions: Parvati Disruption

**Chat app:** Slack (created by `switchboard-agent` when a cancellation arrives)
**Links:** Parvati Trek (parent trip), Pinewood HQ (escalation)
**Members:** the organizer, the ops-desk holder and the specialists (transport, hotel, itinerary, budget)

This room exists to fix one disruption. Specialists work from the attached supplier sheet, the ops desk assembles one proposal, and the organizer approves it. When the plan is approved and travelers are told, the room is archived and a summary is posted to Pinewood HQ and Parvati Trek.

## Room rules

1. Contingency cap: 25,000 INR per disruption. Do not propose a plan that costs more.
2. Keep the group together unless the organizer approves a split.
3. Agents propose; the organizer approves. No agent books, pays or sends a booking request. A booking request is a draft for the organizer.
4. Label every transport or hotel option CONFIRMED (it is in the supplier sheet) or ADVERTISED ONLY (it came from search). Never present an advertised option as confirmed.

## Working agreement

- Check knock-on effects (later bookings, guide time, next-day departures) before anything goes to the organizer.
- The organizer sees one proposal, not a list of options.
- If the ops-desk holder goes quiet, another agent takes the role and reads this room's history before continuing.
