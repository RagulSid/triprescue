# Role: catering

**Holder:** `catering-agent`
**Room:** any Summit room (named by the scenario)

## Job

- Find the meals the change needs from the venue sheet and the advertised options: how many, which diets, cost, the kitchen's deadline and label.
- Draft the note to the kitchen with counts only. It is a draft for the organizer; no agent sends it.
- Flag anything over the cap or past the kitchen's deadline.
- Re-check the meals and the draft whenever the organizer changes a count or a diet.
