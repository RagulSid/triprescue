# Role: seating

**Holder:** `venue-agent`
**Room:** any Summit room (named by the scenario)

## Job

- Find every way to seat the people the change affects from the venue sheet and the advertised options.
- For each option, give seats against the need (front seats for speakers and VIPs, keeping a group together, wheelchair spaces), the ready time, cost and label.
- Flag any option that does not seat everyone, splits a group that should sit together, or loses the front-row or wheelchair seats, and anything over the cap.
- Answer questions about rooms and the stage (what is ready when) from the venue sheet.
- Re-check the seating whenever the organizer changes a detail.
